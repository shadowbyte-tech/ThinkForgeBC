About ResourceX

Industrial materials should be treated as resources
before they are treated as waste.

ResourceX is being built to create digital infrastructure for industrial secondary material exchange.

DEMO DATA
01 — The Problem

Every day, industrial processes generate materials that cannot be immediately reused within the same facility.

These materials — machining turnings, polymer regrind, construction aggregate, glass cullet, textile fibre — accumulate as surplus.

In many cases, a material that is surplus at one facility is a potential input at another.

The problem is not the absence of compatible buyers. It is the absence of the infrastructure to find them, compare them, and evaluate the economic viability of the exchange.

02 — Our Approach

A platform for discovery, matching, and evaluation.

01

DISCOVERY

Make industrial secondary materials visible and searchable by material type, grade, location, and quantity.

02

MATCHING

Not just connecting sellers and buyers, but estimating the net value of each potential exchange after accounting for transport, processing, and handling.

03

EVALUATION

Help buyers and sellers compare options, understand the economics, and negotiate terms.

03 — What We Are Not
  • Not a payment or settlement platform
  • Not a logistics provider
  • Not a material quality verification service
  • Not a regulatory compliance system
  • Not a guaranteed transaction facilitator
ResourceX creates visibility and analytical infrastructure. Commercial terms, logistics, and quality verification remain the responsibility of the parties involved.
