Technical Specification

The Net Value Mathematical Model

DETERMINISTIC FORMULATION

Conventional B2B scrap marketplaces treat secondary materials as consumer commodities with simple spot bids. ResourceX establishes an engineering-grade net yield model:

Net_Value = Gross_Offer - (Distance_km × Freight_Rate_₹/km) - (Base_Processing × Processing_Factor) - Handling_Fee
1. Distance Decay:
Beyond a material-specific transit threshold (e.g., modeled ~140 km sensitivity threshold for turnings in demo simulation), freight costs exceed gross yield premiums.
2. Chemical Compatibility:
A 5-axis spider algorithm weights chemical tolerance, contaminant thresholds, and batch sizing.
3. Seller Sovereignty:
In Material Bank, lots never trigger automated markdowns. Sellers retain full price discretion.
