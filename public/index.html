02 The Problem

Industrial surplus is not waste. It is an undiscovered resource.

Millions of metric tons of high-purity industrial byproducts—machining turnings, engineered polymer regrind, clean mineral slag—are routinely downcycled or discarded.

The failure is not technical chemistry; it is information asymmetry. Generators lack standardized technical fingerprinting, while secondary processors cannot assess contamination risks or freight costs before committing to procurement.
